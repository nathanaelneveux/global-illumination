# Architecture review, 2026-10-24

After the workspace split, the far-LOD work and the NAADF experiments. Each
finding says what was measured or read, and what it asks for. Numbers are M4,
paced headless 1440p unless noted.

## LOD and other engines

- **Rendering side: generic.** Faces come from the host's prepass; when a
  host mesh is not GI's voxels (LOD, smoothing, impostors), traced discovery
  finds the true face through GI's own geometry. It no longer has any distance
  threshold (`e607385`), so it does not depend on any host's LOD layout.
- **Ingestion side: not generic.** `VoxelSource::sample_page` must return
  full-resolution 32³ pages. A host whose LOD stores only coarse data far away
  (asteroids-redux, BVW's LOD example) has to synthesize full resolution or
  stop reporting distant chunks. The BVW connector synthesizes through
  `GiFallbackVoxel`, a per-voxel `&self` call no chunk-scoped generator can
  implement efficiently. Coarse pages ([far-lod-design](far-lod-design.md)) or
  a page-scoped fallback (BVW's own lookup delegate) are the fix.

## Multicrate state

The split holds: core knows no host, the connector owns the BVW material, the
debug crate is optional, the lab owns validation. What is left for the
public-API pass (packaging stage 4):

- **Too much was public** (resolved). `VoxelGiStats` had 34 public fields,
  most of them scheduler internals; it now keeps nine host-facing ones and
  groups the rest under `scheduler`. `VoxelGiSettings` had 17; the eight
  scheduler work bounds moved to the crate-private `SchedulerSettings`.
- **Legacy names** (resolved). `VoxelEnergyField` is now the crate-private
  `ReceiverField`; `EnergyEmitter { spectrum }` is `VoxelGiEmitter { color }`
  in `sources.rs`; `spectrum.rs`'s `Radiance` is `color.rs`'s `FiniteColor`.
- **Material contract.** The connector's material includes core's traversal
  textually (`include_traversal`) and overrides Bevy's lighting through
  naga_oil `override fn`; Bevy 0.20 (WESL) needs the lighting-as-functions
  design (packaging decision 1).
- **Test seam.** `voxel_gi::testing` (`#[doc(hidden)]`) exists only for the
  connector's GPU test.

## Duplicated or vestigial work

- **Sun corner visibility was computed three ways** (resolved): the CPU sky
  cache for world-cache receivers, the view tier per face, and the material's
  fallback for unowned faces. The CPU cache's corners were never displayed; its
  real role was sunlight-change retention events, both for edits whose shadow
  lands far away (a high ceiling opening relights the floor below it) and for a
  moving sun's shadow borders. Both now run on the GPU: edits trace shadow
  footprints into their Light events (`trace_footprints`), and the view tier
  reports its faces' corner flips (`report_sun_flips`, `sun_changes.rs`). The
  cache, sky-only batches, their readbacks and probe sky corners are gone
  (-1.3k lines). Lag behind fixed-sun references on the day cycle fell from
  .020/.031/.039 to .018/.023/.034; the skylight test matched within 1 s and
  was lower after.
- **Display priors (`history.rs`)** are still live: they seed new world-cache
  faces, and the view tier imports them for revealed faces.

## Systems that work against each other

- **Unbounded GPU queue in headless runs** (fixed `03bb292`): with no
  backpressure, GPU overload looked like memory, not frame time.
- **Discovery identity vs raster** (fixed): LOD meshes left faces without
  receivers until traced discovery.
- **Pending geometry vs absent regions:** an early-out that dropped receivers
  for unpublished geometry broke the pending-geometry contract; caught by a
  native test.
- **Static GPU split** between world cache and view tier
  (`world_budget_fraction` 0.25 of the share) rather than demand-based. The
  view trace takes any freed time within its share, but the split itself is
  fixed.

## Assumptions to re-test

- **Per-face receivers capped at 64k** (131k at High) versus NAADF's
  per-pixel samples: at distance faces are about one pixel and the pool
  saturates; reconstruction and reprojection then cost per receiver (8-12 ms
  in the 200-chunk test).
- **World cache radius 64** (re-checked at 5e6afb4, kept): house
  turned/return 400 ms, cave 15/30 s, house peak MB, one headless run each:
  cacheless .058/.069, .018/.013, 862; 32: .0089/.0039, .0104/.0064, 896;
  64: .0087/.0044, .0076/.0046, 976; 96: .0110/.0053, .0073/.0045, 994.
  64 matches 32 on reveal and 96 on the cave; 96 also doubled streaming GI
  main p95 (0.35 -> 0.81 ms). Frame time was equal at all four.
- **CPU construction** of every region (NAADF builds on the GPU), and a CPU
  copy of every mixed region's materials.
- **Full-resolution-only pages** (see LOD above).

## Known issues found this session

- wgpu "Mismatched pop_error_scope call" panic (resolved): it recurred in a
  cold-start baseline run. Bevy builds wgpu without its `std` feature, which
  makes every thread share one error-scope stack, so GI pipeline validation on
  a worker interleaved with Bevy's pipeline cache. The workspace now enables
  wgpu `std` (thread-local scopes); `error_scopes_are_thread_local` reproduces
  the panic without it.
- GI on the BVW performance fork: edit-timing regression in the default gate
  (`asteroids-integration.md` item 9).
- The lab's `lod` scene is still host-bound on crates.io BVW (PostUpdate
  ~94 ms, physics spikes); needs a Tracy capture.
- The intermittent turn frame-0 black spike (about 48k px) recurs.
- Load shadow footprints (introduced and fixed this session, `9842525`):
  conservative footprints for streaming loads cut history around the player;
  house reveal FLIP went .012 to .10 until loads lost their footprints. The
  sunfp gate set had no house run: house belongs in any retention change's
  checks.
